Legal planning

Clear rules for both sides.

This planning page identifies the contractual topics that candidate and employer terms will need to cover.

Planning draft: Final enforceable terms require legal review and acceptance mechanics.

Shared platform terms

Eligibility, account security, acceptable use, intellectual property, service availability, limitations, dispute processes, governing law, suspension, termination, and changes to the service.

Candidate terms

Candidates remain responsible for the truthfulness of submitted information and for reviewing AI-assisted output before use. Candidait should not guarantee interviews, employment, or a specific hiring result.

Employer terms

Employers must be authorized to represent their organization, publish legitimate roles, follow employment and anti-discrimination laws, safeguard candidate information, and use it only for permitted recruiting purposes.

Marketplace boundaries

Candidait provides tools and marketplace infrastructure but does not make the final employment decision. Fees, subscriptions, cancellations, refunds, and any placement-related services require explicit commercial terms.

Trust and enforcement

The service needs reporting, investigation, content removal, account restriction, appeals, and cooperation processes for fraud, impersonation, scraping, harassment, discriminatory conduct, or misuse of confidential candidate information.

Clear rules support a trustworthy marketplace.
