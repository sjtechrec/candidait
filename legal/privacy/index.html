Trust framework

Privacy should be understandable.

This planning page defines the intended privacy model for the Candidait marketplace before a formal policy is reviewed and adopted.

PrinciplesCandidate dataSharingEmployer accessRetentionYour controls
Planning draft: This is a product-design framework, not the final Privacy Policy or legal advice.

Privacy principles

Collect only what is necessary to provide the requested service. Keep private work private. Explain material uses before collection. Limit access by purpose. Delete information when it is no longer needed.

Candidate information

Candidate data may include account information, resumes, professional history, preferences, optimizer projects, applications, communications, and candidate-approved profile information. Sensitive data should not be requested unless a defined feature and lawful purpose require it.

Candidate-to-employer sharing

Optimizer projects, drafts, searches, and saved jobs remain private. Employers receive information only when a candidate applies, opts into discovery, or otherwise authorizes a defined disclosure. A confirmation step should identify what will be shared and with whom.

Employer access and confidentiality

Employer access is limited to verified organizations and authorized team members. Access should be scoped by job and role, logged for review, and prohibited for scraping, resale, unrelated marketing, or non-recruiting use.

Retention and deletion

Each data category requires a documented retention period. Candidate projects should be deletable by the candidate, subject to legitimate security, legal-hold, and employer recordkeeping exceptions. Backups and subprocessors must follow aligned deletion schedules.

User controls

  • Review and correct profile information
  • Change discoverability and blocked organizations
  • Download candidate-controlled information
  • Delete eligible projects and accounts
  • Manage communication preferences
  • Submit applicable privacy-rights requests

Trust is part of the product.
