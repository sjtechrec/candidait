Marketplace standards

Real roles. Clear expectations.

This planning policy defines the minimum trust and quality requirements for jobs published through Candidait.

RequiredProhibitedVerificationLifecycleReporting
Planning draft: This page is a product and moderation framework pending legal review.

Every job must include

  • A real open position and authorized employer
  • Clear title, responsibilities, and qualifications
  • Location and remote or onsite expectations
  • Employment type and compensation where required
  • A valid application method and closing status

Prohibited postings

  • Fake roles or resume-collection schemes
  • Jobs requiring candidates to pay to apply
  • Misleading employer, location, salary, or work-type information
  • Discriminatory or unlawful requirements
  • Unauthorized agency or third-party representation
  • Keyword stuffing, unrelated promotion, or duplicate spam

Employer verification

Jobs remain unpublished until organization identity and poster authorization meet the applicable verification standard. Higher-risk activity may require additional review.

Job lifecycle

Expired, filled, withdrawn, or materially inaccurate roles must be closed promptly. Public job pages and structured data should reflect the current status.

Reporting and enforcement

Candidates need a visible way to report fraud, harassment, discriminatory content, payment demands, impersonation, or data misuse. Candidait should document review, removal, restriction, and appeal procedures.

Real people deserve real opportunities.
