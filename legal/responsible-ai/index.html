AI that knows its place

Evidence before inference.

Candidait should help people communicate and evaluate relevant evidence without fabricating experience or replacing accountable human judgment.

01 / TRUTH

No invented evidence

AI may clarify or reorganize supported facts, but it must not create achievements, metrics, titles, dates, or expertise.

02 / CLARITY

Explain material outputs

Recommendations should identify their purpose, supporting evidence, uncertainty, and limits.

03 / AGENCY

Keep people responsible

Candidates approve their story. Employers remain responsible for hiring decisions and accommodations.

Candidate toolsEmployer toolsProhibited usesGovernance

Candidate-side assistance

Source evidence, analysis, proposed language, and candidate approval should remain visibly distinct. The user must be able to correct extraction errors and reject any recommendation.

Employer-side assistance

Candidate matching must be based on relevant, authorized evidence. Candidait should avoid opaque personality scoring, automatic rejection, protected-characteristic inference, and unsupported claims about potential.

Prohibited uses

  • Fabricating candidate qualifications
  • Inferring protected or highly sensitive traits
  • Ranking based on irrelevant proxies
  • Using private optimizer activity for employer decisions
  • Removing meaningful human review from consequential decisions

Governance requirements

Material systems should support version records, audit events, impact review, accommodation pathways, bias evaluation, incident response, and clear ownership for approvals and overrides.

AI should support the decision, not become the decision.
