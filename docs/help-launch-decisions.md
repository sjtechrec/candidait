# Help workflow and launch decision record

Status: proposed product workflow for review, not an operational policy or contractual commitment.

## 1. Request routing

| Category | Proposed owning function | Minimum initial content | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Technical | Support | Task, error, expected outcome, redacted page path | Do not request credentials or source resumes |
| Privacy | Privacy lead | Request type, data context, recipient if known | Determine own-purpose versus employer-directed processing; coordinate or explain referral |
| Accommodation | Accessibility support | Barrier, requested adjustment or format | No diagnosis required initially; provide independently accessible route |
| Recovery | Account security | Access issue, non-sensitive error | Never disclose records based on case ID; proportionate identity confirmation |
| Appeal | Decision review | Decision, disputed facts, requested outcome | Distinct from employer hiring outcomes; establish review independence |
| Safety | Trust and safety | Listing/incident reference, concern | Restricted sensitive evidence handling and urgent escalation |
| Employer service | Service operations | Agreement/reference, disputed charge or deliverable | Distinct from moderation or account appeal |

These are functional responsibilities, not assertions that staff have been hired or assigned. Name an accountable owner and backup before launch. Legal/privacy/security involvement and response clocks must be classified by actual request, not just the selected category. Do not make a wrong-category selection reset a statutory deadline.

## 2. Lifecycle requirements

Received -> Routing -> Under review -> Information needed (when necessary) -> Outcome provided -> Closed.

- Generate a reference only after durable successful intake, never in a draft preview.
- Supply private confirmation with receipt time, topic, next step, and access to replies.
- Case IDs are identifiers, not bearer credentials. Authorize access and avoid personal data in notification URLs.
- Route misclassified cases internally or explain the correct recipient without losing receipt evidence.
- Case status must distinguish waiting on the user from waiting on Candidait or an employer.
- Establish legal clocks, service targets, escalation ownership, reviewer separation, and exceptions before launch. Do not fabricate fixed public response promises.
- Enable an accessible non-account route for recovery and requests where sign-in is unavailable.
- Restrict medical, biometric, fraud/security, and employer information to authorized handlers. Separate evidence collection from initial intake.
- Define request records, access logs, retention, holds, deletion, closure, challenge, and reopening.
- Internal appeal/support does not automatically suspend external legal deadlines.

## 3. Candidate product decisions

Before asserting these as controls: verify coaching visibility separation, opt-in discovery, exact application payload preview, blocked-employer coverage/limits, profile hiding, application withdrawal, record export/deletion, verification alternatives, and passkey recovery. Do not promise anonymity or guaranteed blocking of an employer.

## 4. Employer product decisions

Define organization onboarding and affiliation checks, team roles/removal, application exports and records preservation, candidate-request coordination, service scope approval, invoicing, and separate service versus platform disputes. Do not automatically delete hiring records when an account or team member leaves.

### User update: October 28, 2026

- Full placement credit replaces the earlier prorated-credit direction when a hire leaves or is terminated for cause before the applicable protection period ends. Detailed qualifying circumstances still need definition; do not equate all layoffs or employer misconduct with qualifying departures.
- Credits should be claimed within one year. The starting event for that claim window and the separate deadline to use an issued credit have not been settled.
- First Hire Free eligibility: employers that have never previously hired through Candidait. Related-entity treatment and other scope details remain open.
- User requested an installment recommendation rather than approving a schedule. Proposed for discussion: Essential 2 equal monthly installments, Protected 3, Premium 6; first due on start date, subsequent payments on monthly anniversaries. Not agreed.
- Unpaid-installment treatment and the amount of a Premium cash refund remain unresolved. Full credit must not silently become a full cash-refund promise.

The table above preserves the earlier baseline; this dated update supersedes its prorated-credit and first-hire eligibility entries. Other final terms require user decisions.
