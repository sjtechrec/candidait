# Policy drafting brief for Candidait LLC

Internal drafting material. Not approved terms, not a privacy notice, and not a substitute for counsel review. Do not link this brief as operative website policy. This brief deliberately does not invent addresses, vendors, retention periods, governing law, liability caps, or dispute clauses.

## Candidate terms draft core

Candidait LLC provides the services identified in the applicable product description. Candidate marketplace access is free under the approved business direction. Users must provide accurate information, respect others' privacy, and use the service for authorized purposes. Resume suggestions and other AI outputs require the user's review; users should approve only factual claims supported by their experience. Career tools do not guarantee an interview, ATS result, placement, or offer.

Separate the license needed to process source documents and deliver services from any permission for employer discovery or applications. Specify the actual visibility and sharing controls, account eligibility, acceptable use, suspension notices, review routes, termination, export, and applicable retention. Training, advertising, resale, and other secondary uses require explicit policy decisions and verified vendor terms rather than a broad perpetual license.

Unresolved drafting clauses: eligibility/age/territories; legal contact/address; actual feature scope; IP/licenses; third-party services; consumer protections; governing law/disputes; disclaimers/liability; updates and acceptance history.

## Employer agreement draft core

Identify Candidait LLC and the contracting organization. Each paid engagement needs an agreed service scope, deliverables, price, and payment schedule. Free employer offers do not obligate a subsequent purchase. A free assessment or playbook does not itself authorize a paid implementation engagement. Employers must use candidate information only for authorized hiring purposes, control team access, and review their job content and decisions.

Separate organization verification, job moderation, and hiring decisions. Establish data-processing roles based on actual purposes, instructions, and services; do not make an employer promise of compliance a substitute for Candidait's responsibilities. Define security, confidentiality, candidate requests, legal holds, export, termination, and vendor arrangements in the appropriate agreement/DPA.

Commercial clauses cannot be finalized until the decisions in help-launch-decisions.md are resolved. Include placement protection and billing mechanics in the agreement itself; the FAQ is not the complete agreement.

## Privacy notice drafting structure

1. Identity and contact: Candidait LLC; verified privacy address/contact still required.
2. Actual collection at current stage: hosting/server logs, cookies, analytics, inquiries and other actual processing; inventory must establish facts even for a coming-soon website.
3. Candidate contexts: private documents/coaching, discoverable profiles, applications, security and support.
4. Employer contexts: organization/team records, applicant processing, interview/decision records, service/billing records.
5. Purpose and role per category; legal bases where required; actual recipients and countries.
6. AI/identity and other providers: actual inputs, outputs, access, training and retention settings, contracts.
7. Retention by record category and trigger; lawful exceptions, holds, backups and deletion propagation.
8. Rights and choices under applicable law, request methods, verification, representatives where applicable, handling of employer-directed records, complaint and review routes.
9. Cookie/marketing choices and applicable opt-out signals, based on actual technologies.
10. Security summary supported by implemented controls, policy changes and dated versions.

Do not convert this outline into an affirmative privacy promise until the data inventory is verified.

## AI disclosure drafting structure

For each released feature state intended use, permitted context, data used and recipient/provider, retention/training practices, output limitations, evidence/review controls, and challenge or alternative process. Distinguish private candidate assistance from employer recommendations, screening, ranking, or rejection. Determine applicable assessment/audit/notice requirements before enabling hiring tools. Do not claim bias-free, EEOC-certified, or universally compliant services.

## Supporting operational documents

Employer DPA and subprocessor inventory; actual job-posting/acceptable-use rules; accessibility and alternative routes; identity notice and provider-specific consent when required; data retention/hold schedule; rights-request procedure; incident handling; account recovery; appeal independence and recordkeeping; contract and notice acceptance/version history.
