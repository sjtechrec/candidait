# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Candidait welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Showing empathy, patience, and respect.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing discussion on what is best for the project and its community.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing when appropriate, and learning from mistakes.
- Respecting privacy and handling sensitive information carefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or demeaning comments.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, insults, or sustained disruption of project discussions.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and whenever someone is publicly representing the project or its community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal details.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect reporters' privacy where possible, and respond in a manner proportionate to the circumstances. Possible actions include a private warning, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is prohibited.
