Account recovery

Get ready to restore access safely.

Candidait is coming soon. You are previewing Help content for the future service. Accounts, applications, support intake, and verification are not active here. This preview does not submit requests or collect documents. Contact channels and final service procedures will be confirmed before launch.

Identify the problem

  • Lost access to a passkey device or credential manager.
  • An expired sign-in link or unavailable account email.
  • A possible account takeover or unfamiliar sign-in.
  • An account restriction, which needs an appeal rather than bypassing a security check.

Safe first steps

  • Check whether the credential is available on another device or credential manager you already use.
  • Navigate directly to Candidait rather than following an unexpected recovery message.
  • Do not give passwords, passkeys, one-time codes, or full identity documents to someone offering help.
  • Avoid creating duplicate accounts to work around an identity restriction.

Prepare a recovery request

Use the recovery request preview. Describe the problem and any non-sensitive error message. In the operating service, a verified private route will be needed to identify the account and confirm control without exposing it to an unauthorized person. Recovery cannot promise restoration before that check.

If you suspect compromise

Secure the affected email account through its provider and review access to devices or credential managers you control. Preserve relevant notices. Never approve an unexpected authentication prompt. Describe the suspected compromise through the recovery route; do not send the suspicious link or token unredacted.
