Candidait Help

Frequently asked questions

A straightforward guide to what is planned and what is available today.

Candidait is coming soon. These pages explain the planned experience. Support intake, accounts, applications, and verification are not available on this site yet. Nothing on these pages submits a request.
What is Candidait?

Candidait is being developed as an employer-funded talent marketplace with candidate career tools and employer hiring services.

Is marketplace access free for candidates?

The business direction is free candidate marketplace access. Employers fund the marketplace through optional paid services. The marketplace is not open yet.

Can I apply for jobs or upload my resume today?

No. This website is currently a coming-soon site. Accounts, applications, document processing, and production AI search are not connected.

Which employer services are planned?

Talent Operations Audit, Outplacement Services, Talent Acquisition Playbook, and Contingency Search are part of the planned offering. Embedded Recruiter is a future service. Availability and scope will be explained before an offer opens.

What does First Hire Free mean?

It is intended as a genuinely free first placement with no obligation to purchase again. Eligibility and scope are still being defined. It is not available to claim on this website yet.

Will employers see my private resume work?

The intended design separates private resume coaching, an optionally discoverable profile, and applications you choose to submit. These are planned boundaries, not a statement that an account system is operating today.

Will AI make hiring decisions?

AI tools are planned, but employer screening and ranking capabilities require separate review before release. No production AI hiring-decision system is operating on this site.

Can I use a VPN?

The planned policy permits VPN use. Before submitting an application while using a VPN, candidates will need additional identity verification and a passkey. Provider selection, alternative routes, and the verification experience are still being developed.

Do I need to accept optional cookies?

The intended design provides alternatives to optional remembered-device and personalization cookies. Actual cookie choices and notices will need to reflect the technologies used when features launch.

Where can I raise an issue?

Read the Contact page for current availability and safe preparation. A verified support contact and submission route have not been published yet.
