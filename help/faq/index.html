Candidait Help

Frequently asked questions

Practical answers for candidates and employers.

Candidait is coming soon. You are previewing Help content for the future service. Accounts, applications, support intake, and verification are not active here. This preview does not submit requests or collect documents. Contact channels and final service procedures will be confirmed before launch.
What is Candidait?

Candidait is an employer-funded talent marketplace connecting candidate career preparation with employer hiring tools and recruiting services. Candidates can use career tools and pursue opportunities; employers can use optional paid services to support hiring.

Do candidates pay to access the marketplace?

Candidate marketplace access is free under the business model. Employer-funded services support the marketplace. Review the scope of any separate offer before accepting it; do not assume a message asking a candidate for a placement fee is legitimate.

How do I start a useful job search?

Describe the role, relevant skills, preferred location, remote-work needs, employment type, and compensation expectations. Separate requirements from preferences. Review the actual job description and employer information before applying rather than relying only on a recommendation.

How should I use the Resume Optimizer?

Start with an accurate source resume and a target job description. Check extracted roles, dates, and achievements; review suggested changes against your evidence. Approve only statements you can support, then inspect the exported document before sharing it. AI suggestions can be incomplete or incorrect.

Does a stronger resume guarantee interviews?

No. Resume improvements can make experience clearer, but do not guarantee an ATS result, interview, placement, or job offer. Do not add unsupported skills, titles, metrics, or qualifications.

How are private work, profiles, and applications different?

Private resume preparation is distinct from information selected for employer discovery and information submitted to a particular employer. Check the recipient and the content before sharing. Hiding a profile, withdrawing an application, and deleting a document are different actions.

How should employers use AI-generated content?

Review job requirements, compensation, location eligibility, and suggested interview questions before using them. Check relevance and accuracy, avoid discriminatory criteria, and do not treat a generated summary as a substitute for reading the candidate’s authorized information.

Which employer services are included in the business direction?

Talent Operations Audit, Outplacement Services, Talent Acquisition Playbook, and Contingency Search. Free offers lead to optional paid work with a defined scope. Embedded Recruiter is a later service. Availability and engagement details belong in the applicable service offer.

What is First Hire Free?

The offer is intended as a free first placement with no obligation to purchase another placement. Eligibility and scope must be stated before the offer opens; they are not settled by this preview.

What are the paid contingency options?

The proposed tiers are Essential at 25% of annual base salary, with prorated placement credit for qualifying departures during a 60-day protection period; Protected at 30%, with prorated placement credit for qualifying departures during a 90-day protection period; and Premium at 35%, with prorated placement credit or money back for qualifying departures during a 180-day protection period. Monthly installments are planned. The start of each protection period, qualifying departures, claim procedures, proration, credit/refund eligibility, and payment schedules will be defined in the service agreement. First Hire Free eligibility and scope also remain to be finalized.

Can I apply using a VPN?

Yes under the planned policy, with additional identity verification and a passkey before submitting an application while using a VPN. If the process presents an accessibility or document-coverage barrier, follow the verification support guidance rather than trying to bypass the check.

What does a verification badge mean?

It should describe the specific check performed. Identity, employer affiliation, organization verification, and experience checks are different. No single badge proves every claim or guarantees a safe job.

How do I recognize a suspicious opportunity?

Look closely at requests to pay fees, buy equipment through an unfamiliar seller, share banking information early, or move immediately to an unverified channel. Check the organization and contact independently. Save the listing and message references and avoid sending more money or information while investigating.

What should I do about an error or disputed decision?

For a broken task, record the page, error, time, and expected outcome. For a platform decision, identify the notice and explain the disputed facts. For an employer hiring decision, use the employer’s published route. The Contact and Appeals pages help you prepare the right message.

How do optional cookies relate to access?

The design direction separates essential security/session functions from optional personalization and remembered-device choices. Declining optional cookies should not be confused with failing identity verification. Actual choices and disclosures must match the technologies used at launch.

Employer services: free scope and paid work

  • Talent Operations Audit: the free offer is assessment, findings, and recommendations. Implementation is optional paid work with a separate scope. The agentic assessment capability is still planned.
  • Outplacement: free candidate access and guidance support exiting employees. Employer-brand analysis and remediation remain a tentative paid follow-on, not an included commitment.
  • Talent Acquisition Playbook: the free offer is a framework, templates, and guidance. Adaptation and implementation are optional paid engagements.
  • Contingency Search: First Hire Free is intended as a genuine free first placement with no obligation to buy again. Eligibility and scope need final terms. Later placements use the selected paid tier.
  • Embedded Recruiter: a future offer; introductory scope, availability, and pricing are not settled.

Example: placement fee versus installments

For a $100,000 annual base salary, the proposed total placement fees are $25,000 for Essential, $30,000 for Protected, and $35,000 for Premium. These are total fees, not monthly charges. Monthly amounts cannot be calculated until the installment count and schedule are agreed. A placement credit and a cash refund are different remedies. Eligible uses of a credit, refund conditions, and treatment of unpaid installments will be defined in the service agreement.

Employer verification and team access

Before publishing or accessing candidate information, the operating service must establish how an organization and its representatives are verified. Describe company-name changes, affiliation mismatches, duplicate organizations, and joining an existing team through employer support. Do not share another team member’s credentials. Team access should be limited to the relevant hiring purpose, and removed when responsibilities end.

Applications, exports, and preservation

A candidate correction or deletion request can concern both platform information and employer hiring records. Employers need a defined process for applicable record retention, legal holds, authorized exports, and requests forwarded by Candidait. An account closure or departing team member should not trigger indiscriminate record deletion.

Service dispute or platform appeal?

Use employer service support for an invoice discrepancy, disputed deliverable, placement credit, or refund question. Identify the agreement, disputed item, and requested resolution without including bank details. Use the platform appeal route for a removed listing or restricted account. Candidait cannot settle an invoice dispute merely by restoring platform access.
