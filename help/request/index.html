The route depends on the issue


    

  • 
Technical support: describe the task, error, and expected outcome.
    • 

  • 
Privacy: identify the data and the request, such as access, correction, or deletion. Candidait-held records and employer-held applications may need coordinated handling.
    • 

  • 
Accommodations: describe the barrier and a helpful adjustment. A diagnosis is not needed in the initial request.
    • 

  • 
Recovery: explain the access problem without disclosing credentials. A working sign-in must not be a prerequisite for asking for help.
    • 

  • 
Appeals: identify the decision and the facts you want reviewed.
    • 

  • 
Safety: identify the listing or incident; avoid sending sensitive evidence in an initial message.
    • 

  • 
Employer services: identify the engagement and disputed deliverable, invoice, or placement term.
    • 







Before any real submission


Check the recipient and remove unnecessary personal information. Do not include credentials, authentication codes, government IDs, biometric images, medical records, or payment details. Any necessary evidence needs a separate, verified secure route. Do not put private account details in a URL.






What happens next



Read the proposed request lifecycle. The future service needs confirmation, a private reference, replies, and escalation before real intake opens.
