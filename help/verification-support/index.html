Verification support

Identity checks should have a clear purpose and a safer path.

Candidait is coming soon. You are previewing Help content for the future service. Accounts, applications, support intake, and verification are not active here. This preview does not submit requests or collect documents. Contact channels and final service procedures will be confirmed before launch.

Understand what is being checked

  • Identity: whether a person matches the identity presented for the check.
  • Employer affiliation: whether a representative is connected with an organization.
  • Organization: information about the business behind an employer account.
  • Experience or qualifications: individual claims, which are separate from identity.

A completed identity check does not prove every resume claim, guarantee a job is genuine, or endorse someone’s conduct. Read the explanation attached to a verification label rather than treating all labels as interchangeable.

VPNs and application verification

VPN use is permitted in the planned candidate experience. Applying while using a VPN requires additional identity verification and binding a passkey before submission. Email or phone control alone is not identity proof. The verification provider, document handling, and alternative review process require confirmation before launch.

Before starting a check

  1. Start from a verified Candidait page, not an unsolicited message or an unfamiliar link.
  2. Read the notice explaining the purpose, the provider, the information requested, and the relevant data practices.
  3. Check that the device and browser support the required steps.
  4. If the process creates an access barrier, ask about an alternative before submitting additional sensitive information.

Troubleshoot a failed or incomplete check

  • Page or camera problem: note the error, browser, and device. Check the requested permission and whether another application is using the camera.
  • Document problem: check the provider’s supported document guidance. Do not alter an image or change information to force a match.
  • Name mismatch: describe whether the issue involves a professional name, changed name, or a document/account discrepancy through the verified support route.
  • Interrupted session: keep the reference number and look for a status update before starting repeated submissions.
  • Accessibility or coverage problem: describe the barrier and ask about an alternative review route.

Passkeys and account safety

A passkey helps authenticate access to an account; it does not independently establish the accuracy of a resume. Keep access to the device or credential manager you use. Never disclose a passkey, password, or one-time code to a person claiming to provide support. If you lose access, use the published recovery route rather than creating duplicate identities to bypass a check.

Request a review

Describe the failed step, its reference number, and why the result appears incorrect. Do not attach government IDs or biometric images to a general message. Ask for the appropriate secure route if supporting information is needed. See Appeals and Accommodations.

Lost access or need review?

Account recovery addresses lost credentials. The appeal draft preview addresses a disputed verification-related decision. Neither route should require repeating the same inaccessible step to ask for help.
