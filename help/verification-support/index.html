Verification support

Identity checks should have a clear purpose and a safer path.

Candidait is coming soon. These pages explain the planned experience. Support intake, accounts, applications, and verification are not available on this site yet. Nothing on these pages submits a request.

Verification is not live

No identity verification provider or verification intake is connected to this website. Do not upload or email an identity document in response to these pages.

The planned VPN policy

Candidates may use a VPN. The planned application process requires additional identity verification and passkey binding before a candidate submits an application while using a VPN. The provider flow and alternative review options remain under development.

Understand verification labels

Identity verification, employer verification, and checks of experience or qualifications are different. Confirming identity does not establish that every resume claim is accurate or that a job is safe. Badge meanings will need to explain the specific check performed.

If a future check fails

  • Record the error message or reference number without copying sensitive documents.
  • Describe any access barrier or document-coverage problem.
  • Use the verified support route published with that feature.
  • Ask about an alternative or review route when available.

Protect your account

Never disclose a passkey, password, or one-time code to someone claiming to provide support. Only start verification through a confirmed Candidait route. See Contact and Accommodations for related guidance.
