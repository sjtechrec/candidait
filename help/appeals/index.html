Appeals and decision reviews

Prepare a clear explanation when you disagree with a platform decision.

Candidait is coming soon. You are previewing Help content for the future service. Accounts, applications, support intake, and verification are not active here. This preview does not submit requests or collect documents. Contact channels and final service procedures will be confirmed before launch.

Which decisions belong here?

Use this guidance to prepare a challenge to a Candidait platform decision, such as an account restriction, a removed job post, or verification-related access. For inaccurate personal information, use Privacy requests. For a broken feature or technical error, use Contact. For an employer’s application or hiring decision, use that employer’s published recruiting contact or review process. This Appeals page covers Candidait platform decisions.

Prepare your appeal

  1. Identify the decision. Include the notice, reference number, and date if you have them.
  2. Explain exactly what you believe is incorrect or missing. Separate facts from assumptions.
  3. Describe relevant context, such as an account compromise, mistaken organization association, or access barrier.
  4. State the outcome you are asking to have reviewed, such as correction of information or reconsideration of a restriction.
  5. List relevant evidence without attaching sensitive identity documents or unrelated personal records.

Example appeal

Decision: My employer listing was removed.
Reference: The reference shown in the decision notice.
Reason for review: The organization name in the notice differs from the organization I represent.
Relevant context: A recent company name change.
Requested outcome: Review the organization association and explain what evidence is needed.

What a useful review should resolve

A review should address the specific decision and relevant corrections. Possible outcomes include keeping the decision, changing it, or seeking further information. The appeal itself does not guarantee reinstatement, publication, verification, or a hiring outcome. Final review procedures and communication channels must be confirmed before launch.

While a decision is being reviewed

  • Keep the original notice, relevant correspondence, and reference numbers.
  • Describe new information by reference to the original issue instead of submitting conflicting accounts.
  • Do not create another account or repost content to evade a restriction.
  • Do not alter evidence or send confidential information about other people.
  • If an access barrier prevents you from preparing a request, explain the barrier and the adjustment that would help.

Employer hiring decisions

An employer controls its own application and hiring decisions. Ask the employer through its published contact or review process about its decision. A Candidait platform appeal cannot require an employer to offer an interview or job. If the concern also involves a suspicious listing, discriminatory content, or misuse of the platform, describe that separate platform concern using Contact guidance.

Privacy and other routes

For inaccurate personal information or a data request, read Privacy requests. For a failed identity check, read Verification support. These help routes do not replace any independent rights or external remedies that may apply.
