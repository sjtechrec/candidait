Appeals and decision reviews

Prepare a clear explanation when you disagree with a platform decision.

Candidait is coming soon. These pages explain the planned experience. Support intake, accounts, applications, and verification are not available on this site yet. Nothing on these pages submits a request.

Current availability

Account restrictions, verification decisions, and an appeals submission system are not operating on this site yet. This page explains the intended distinction between platform reviews and employer decisions.

Candidait platform decisions

A future review route should identify the decision being challenged, explain what information is relevant, and provide a way to request review. Potential topics include account restrictions, posting removals, and verification-related access. Final procedures will be published with those features.

Employer hiring decisions

An employer’s application or hiring decision is separate from a Candidait platform decision. Use the employer’s published contact or review process for questions about its decision. This page cannot reverse an employer’s hiring decision.

What to prepare

  • The decision notice or reference number, if one exists.
  • The date and a concise explanation of what appears incorrect.
  • Relevant corrections or context, with sensitive details removed.
  • Any accessibility barrier affecting your ability to seek review.

What this page does

This page does not submit an appeal, promise a response time, or guarantee an outcome. See Contact for current availability and Privacy requests for correction or personal-data questions.
