Privacy requests

Understand which information your request concerns.

Identify the information involved

  • Private career work: source resumes, target jobs, coaching conversations, analysis, suggested edits, and exports.
  • Discoverable profile: the profile information and visibility choices used for employer discovery.
  • Submitted applications: the information sent to a named employer and records created in that employer’s hiring process.
  • Account and service records: contact details, preferences, security information, support correspondence, and applicable billing records.

Choose the request that fits

  • Access or export: describe the information or period you want to understand or receive.
  • Correction: identify the inaccurate field, the proposed correction, and why it is inaccurate.
  • Deletion: distinguish a specific document or project from an entire account.
  • Visibility or sharing: say whether you want to change future discovery, stop sharing a profile, or ask about a particular recipient.
  • Processing question or objection: identify the use of information you want explained or reviewed.

Prepare a privacy request

  1. State the request type and data context without attaching the underlying sensitive documents.
  2. Identify the relevant account through the verified request route when it is available. Do not publish account details.
  3. Name an employer or recipient if the request concerns an application or shared profile.
  4. Give a useful date range or reference number and describe the outcome you are seeking.
  5. Tell us if an access barrier affects your ability to use the request route.

Visibility, withdrawal, and deletion are different

Hiding a profile concerns future discovery. Withdrawing an application concerns participation in a hiring process. Deleting private resume work concerns a separate set of records. None of these actions should be assumed to erase copies already received by an employer. Specify which outcome you want so the request can be directed appropriately.

Employer-held applications

An employer may hold hiring records independently of your candidate account. Include the employer’s name when asking about a submitted application, and consult its privacy notice and contact route. Candidait-held information and employer-controlled records may require different handling.

Retention and identity checks

Access and deletion requests can involve identity confirmation and exceptions for security, legal preservation, or other applicable requirements. Available rights depend on the context and applicable law. Do not send an ID as an initial request. Ask why additional information is needed and use only a verified, appropriate channel.

What to keep

Keep a copy of your request, its date, any reference number, and the response. If the response appears incomplete, explain what is missing and refer to the original request. Contact guidance explains safe message preparation; Appeals explains how to describe a disputed platform decision.
