Privacy requests

Understand which information your request concerns.

Candidait is coming soon. These pages explain the planned experience. Support intake, accounts, applications, and verification are not available on this site yet. Nothing on these pages submits a request.

Three different data contexts

  • Private career work: resumes and coaching projects used for your own preparation.
  • Discoverable profile: information you choose to make available to employers.
  • Submitted application: information sent to a particular employer for its hiring process.

Describe the request

A request might concern access, correction, an export, deletion, profile visibility, or questions about processing. Available rights and exceptions depend on the circumstances and applicable law. Identify the data context and the organization involved without including the underlying documents.

Employer application records

An employer may separately hold an application or hiring records. Closing a Candidait account or hiding a profile would not necessarily remove copies already received by an employer. A future request process must distinguish Candidait-held data from employer-controlled records and account for applicable preservation requirements.

Current availability

There is no privacy-request intake form or authenticated privacy center on this site yet. See Contact for current contact availability. This page does not create or submit a request.

Identity and safe handling

Do not publish your resume, ID, account credentials, or someone else’s information to make a request. Any future identity confirmation should use a verified, proportionate process rather than asking for sensitive documents through public channels.
