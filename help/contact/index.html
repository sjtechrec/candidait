What to prepare

  • The page address and a short description of your question.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • The date and approximate time, if relevant.
  • A preferred accessible way to communicate, if needed.

Keep sensitive information private

Do not send passwords, passkeys, authentication codes, government IDs, biometric images, payment details, or a full resume through public channels or GitHub issues. If a screenshot helps, remove personal information first.
