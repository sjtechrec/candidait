Write a useful support message

  1. Give the issue a specific title, such as “Resume export is missing my latest changes.”
  2. Explain what you were trying to do, what happened, and what you expected.
  3. Include the page address with sign-in links, access tokens, and personal information removed, approximate time, and any non-sensitive error or reference number.
  4. Mention steps you already tried and whether the issue happens consistently.
  5. Describe your preferred communication format or an access barrier, if relevant.

Example message

Topic: Resume export
Task: Export my approved resume as a document.
Issue: The export omits the last section I approved.
Expected: All approved sections in the exported file.
Context: Redacted page address, approximate time, browser, and a redacted error message, if available.

Report a suspicious job or message

Include the job or company page address, the message reference, and a short explanation of the concern. Examples include requests for payment, impersonation, misleading job details, or pressure to share sensitive information. Preserve relevant messages; avoid sending money or additional documents while checking the situation. A verification label is not a guarantee that a job or person is safe.

Keep your message safe

Do not include passwords, passkeys, one-time codes, full payment details, government IDs, biometric images, or medical records in an initial support message. Redact screenshots and remove unrelated personal information. Remove sign-in links, access tokens, and personal information from copied page addresses. Do not post private issues in public GitHub discussions or issues.

Contact channels

The support address and request channel are launch details still to be confirmed. No submission channel is active in this preview. Use only contact details published on Candidait’s website once available; verify unexpected requests before sharing information.

Questions for an employer

For an interview schedule, application outcome, accommodation in an employer’s selection process, or employer-held records, use the employer’s published contact. Include the role and application reference, but avoid resending a complete application unless requested through an appropriate channel.
