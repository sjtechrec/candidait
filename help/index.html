A quick guide to the right route

  • Something is not working: record the page, error, and expected result. Start with Contact.
  • Your information is inaccurate or you want a copy or deletion: start with Privacy requests.
  • A process creates an access barrier: describe the task and the adjustment you need. Start with Accommodations.
  • You cannot complete an identity check: use Verification support.
  • You disagree with a platform decision: use Appeals.
  • You need to find a page: open the Sitemap.
