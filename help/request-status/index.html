Candidait Help

After you request help

Understand confirmation, review, replies, and escalation.

Candidait is coming soon. You are previewing Help content for the future service. Accounts, applications, support intake, and verification are not active here. This preview does not submit requests or collect documents. Contact channels and final service procedures will be confirmed before launch.

Proposed service workflow

This is the workflow being designed for the future service. It is not an active case tracker, response-time commitment, or published service-level agreement.

  • Received: a successful submission should provide a confirmation and private case reference. A draft is not submitted. If submission fails or confirmation is missing, do not assume the request was received; use the published follow-up route to check.
  • Routing: the request is assigned by topic. Privacy, security, access, and employer-service questions need appropriately restricted handling.
  • Under review: the assigned reviewer evaluates the issue and relevant information.
  • Information needed: the response should identify what is missing, why it is needed, and an appropriate way to provide it.
  • Outcome provided: the response should explain the result, relevant limitations, and next steps.
  • Closed: the record should explain why the request closed and how to challenge an unresolved issue.

Keep replies together

Use the case reference when following up. Add corrections and new information to the existing conversation so the reviewer can see the history. A case reference alone must not grant access to private messages. Do not post it publicly.

If the answer does not resolve the issue

Describe the unanswered question or disputed facts and request further review. For a Candidait restriction or moderation decision, use the appeal route. For a service or billing issue, use employer service support. A different reviewer and escalation ownership need to be established before launch.

If you cannot sign in

Use account recovery guidance. The future service needs a verified alternative to an account-only inbox, with proportionate identity checks before disclosing private records.

External deadlines

An internal support request or appeal does not necessarily pause external complaint or legal filing deadlines. Do not assume that you must finish Candidait’s process before considering other available routes. Requirements depend on the issue and jurisdiction.
