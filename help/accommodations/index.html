Accessibility and accommodations

Help should work for different abilities, devices, and circumstances.

Candidait is coming soon. These pages explain the planned experience. Support intake, accounts, applications, and verification are not available on this site yet. Nothing on these pages submits a request.

Website access

If a page is difficult to use, note its address, the task you were trying to complete, and the barrier you encountered. You may describe the browser or assistive technology involved if helpful. You do not need to include a diagnosis or medical documents in an initial description.

Planned alternatives

We intend to provide accessible routes for account access, verification, and applications, including a way to request an alternative when a process creates a barrier. Those workflows are not operating yet.

Employer hiring processes

An accommodation for an employer’s interview or selection process should be directed through that employer’s published accommodation contact. Candidait platform support and an employer’s hiring process are different routes.

How to prepare a request

  • Describe the task and the barrier.
  • Explain an adjustment or communication format that would help, if you know.
  • Avoid including medical records or unnecessary sensitive information.

Contact availability

A Candidait accommodation contact has not been published yet. See Contact for support availability. No accommodation request is submitted by viewing this page.
