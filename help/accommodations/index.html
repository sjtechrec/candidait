Accessibility and accommodations

Help should work for different abilities, devices, and circumstances.

Candidait is coming soon. You are previewing Help content for the future service. Accounts, applications, support intake, and verification are not active here. This preview does not submit requests or collect documents. Contact channels and final service procedures will be confirmed before launch.

Describe the barrier, not your medical history

An access request can focus on the task and the obstacle. For example: “I use keyboard navigation and cannot reach the submit control,” or “The timed step does not give me enough time to use my assistive technology.” You do not need to include a diagnosis or medical records in your initial message.

Examples of adjustments to discuss

  • A text-based explanation instead of an audio-only instruction.
  • An accessible document or communication format.
  • An alternative to a task that depends on a particular device, camera, or interaction method.
  • Additional time or a way to pause a time-sensitive step.
  • An alternative review route when automated verification creates an access barrier.

Prepare an accessibility report

  1. Identify the page and the task you were trying to complete.
  2. Describe the barrier and where it occurs.
  3. If helpful, include your browser, device, or assistive technology and a redacted screenshot.
  4. Describe an adjustment that would help, if you know one. You can also ask to discuss alternatives.
  5. State your preferred communication format without adding unnecessary sensitive information.

Verification barriers

If verification fails because of document coverage, camera access, assistive technology, or another barrier, describe that issue separately from an identity mismatch. Do not repeatedly upload documents through unverified routes. Read Verification support for a troubleshooting checklist.

Employer interviews and selection

Requests concerning an employer’s interview, assessment, schedule, or workplace should use that employer’s published accommodation contact. Identify the role and the step that creates a barrier. A website accessibility issue and an employer selection-process request can both exist; describe each so they reach the right organization.

Keep control of sensitive information

Explain the adjustment you need before sending medical evidence. If further information is requested, ask what is necessary, who will receive it, and how to provide it securely. Avoid including medical information in a resume, public profile, or general support screenshot.
