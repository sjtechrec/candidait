Privacy & visibility

Private is the starting point.

Resume drafts, optimizer notes, job searches, and saved jobs remain private. Creating an optimizer project never makes you searchable.

Your visibility choices

  • Private: only you can see your profile
  • Application only: shared when you apply
  • Discoverable: visible to verified employers
  • Discoverable with blocked organizations

What employers never receive automatically

  • Private drafts and optimizer history
  • Jobs you viewed, saved, or searched for
  • Unapproved AI suggestions
  • Information outside your sharing selection
